1. DATA QUALITY & SENSOR HEALTH (PACKET LOSS AUDIT)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
• Missing Telemetry Rate: Approximately 2.03% of all telemetry records exhibit missing
  sensor measurements across temperature (3,637), humidity (3,551), pressure (3,635),
  vibration (3,655), and power consumption (3,548).
• Uniform Drop Pattern: The missingness is uniformly distributed across time and assets,
  indicating transient wireless gateway dropouts and packet buffer overruns rather
  than prolonged hardware sensor failures.
• Extreme Anomaly Signatures: 142 extreme mechanical vibration shock events (>3.0 g up
  to 5.38 g) and morning startup electrical power spikes (>100 kWh up to 198.28 kWh)
  were detected, characteristic of pump cavitation and compressor locked-rotor surges.

2. ASSET-SPECIFIC THERMODYNAMIC & MECHANICAL DYNAMICS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
• Chillers (Refrigeration Loops):
  - Maintain low operating temperatures (mean 6.01°C, median 5.99°C, range -5.0°C to 16.4°C),
    consistent with chilled water supply loop setpoints.
  - Heaviest energy consumers in the building: mean power draw of 42.80 kWh, accounting
    for 58.20% of total facility electricity consumption (1,416,840 kWh).

• Air Handling Units (AHUs - Air Distribution):
  - Deliver conditioned discharge air at a mean temperature of 18.00°C (std 3.49°C).
  - Account for 21.59% of total facility energy (mean draw 18.00 kWh).

• Pumps (Hydronic Fluid Circulation):
  - Exhibit the highest baseline mechanical vibration (mean 0.61 g, max 5.38 g) and elevated
    temperatures (mean 30.08°C) due to continuous motor heat dissipation and condenser water return.
  - Account for 17.49% of total facility energy (mean draw 13.10 kWh).

• Energy Meters & Environmental Sensors (Monitoring Baseline):
  - Steady low internal electrical draw (~3.08 kWh) reflecting ambient room conditions (23.2°C).

3. TEMPORAL DYNAMICS & THE 'PHANTOM LOAD' BASELOAD INEFFICIENCY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
• Strict Workday Occupancy Curve: Building occupancy follows a sharp step profile:
  - Occupied Window (08:00 - 19:00): Average 19.8 to 20.4 occupants.
  - Unoccupied Window (20:00 - 07:00): Drops to 0.0 to 0.8 occupants.
• The 65.5% Nighttime Baseload Trap: While occupancy drops to near zero, total building
  power consumption only declines from 16.91 kWh to 10.92 kWh.
• Unmodulated Night Operations: Chillers continue drawing 41.2 kWh at night (90.3% of
  daytime peak 45.8 kWh), and pumps draw 12.8 kWh (93.4% of peak 13.7 kWh).
• Inefficiency Diagnosis: HVAC plant chillers and hydronic pumps run continuously unthrottled
  throughout the night without automated temperature setbacks or variable flow scheduling.

4. OPERATIONAL MODE ENERGY WASTE (CHILLER IDLE LEAKAGE)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
• Chiller Standby Penalty: Chillers in 'Idle' mode consume an average of 41.84 kWh,
  which is 90.9% of their active 'Cooling' load (46.02 kWh).
• AHU Standby Penalty: AHUs in 'Idle' mode consume 16.18 kWh (81.6% of active cooling 19.82 kWh).
• Physical Cause: Compressors remain energized in unloaded bypass states, constant-speed
  auxiliary pumps remain running, and crankcase heaters run unabated during standby.

5. EQUIPMENT RELIABILITY & FAILURE MECHANISMS (ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
• Overall Fleet Fault Incidence: 2.04% (3,636 fault occurrences across 178,560 intervals).
• Vibration is the Overwhelming Failure Driver: Machine learning feature importance
  demonstrates that mechanical vibration accounts for 71.84% of predictive importance
  (Pearson correlation r = +0.342), far exceeding thermal or electrical metrics.
• Non-Linear Vibration Risk Thresholds:
  - Normal Baseline (< 0.55 g): Fault probability is 1.48% - 1.68%.
  - Warning Threshold (0.71 g - 0.87 g): Fault probability rises to 2.77%.
  - High Alert Threshold (0.87 g - 1.02 g): Fault probability rises to 3.33% (2.2x increase).
  - Critical Alarm (> 1.02 g up to 5.38 g): Fault probability surges to 5.35% (3.7x baseline).
• Physical Meaning: Elevated vibration indicates impending bearing spalling, shaft
  misalignment, impeller imbalance, and fluid cavitation in pumps and chillers.
